Choose a situation and use the target idiom naturally. Give reasons, react to your partner and keep the conversation going.

    Final challenge: The school café

    Your class is opening a café at the school fair. Create a 6–8-line conversation and agree on a plan. Use at least three different idioms naturally, then perform it for another pair.

    Partner A · The organiser
    Your class has a small budget and must cover its costs. The new coffee machine is far too expensive. Suggest a cheaper plan.
    Partner B · The ideas person
    You spent your own allowance already. Suggest borrowing equipment and discuss offering one small treat free to attract customers.

    Talking points: What should you borrow or buy? How will you cover costs? What could you offer free? What would change if a sponsor gave you plenty of extra money?
